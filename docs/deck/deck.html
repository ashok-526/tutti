TuttiThe problem
I · The problem

Dinner is an orchestra
with no conductor.

Three dishes means three timers, and you have one pair of hands. Timers beep, but they can't tell you which dish, what to do, or how long you have.

It's hardest on new cooks, on anyone with ADHD time blindness, and on anyone with flour on their hands.

VI · Life happens

Running late?
The score rewrites itself.

+1 minute
Done

Anything already on the heat stays put. Everything not yet started is re-planned on the spot, within each dish's tolerances: how long the rice can rest, how early the salad can be dressed.

Re-scored. The final chord moves 0:30 later.
VII / XII
TuttiFinale & encore
VII · Finale & encore

Tutti.

Every dish, together.

When the last dish is ready, the whole orchestra plays the final chord. Then the encore plays tonight's dinner back as a short song, each leitmotif entering in the order you cooked it.
